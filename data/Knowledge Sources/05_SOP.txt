1. Overview
This document describes how our customer service team handles inbound enquiries today. We receive enquiries primarily by email through our Gmail inbox. All replies are sent manually by a CS team member after checking the relevant reference documents.
We currently have 3 CS staff sharing the inbox. There is no automated routing or AI assistance. Every email is read, assessed, and replied to by a human.

3. Step-by-Step: How to Handle an Enquiry
Follow these steps for every inbound email:

	•	Read the email fully. Identify what the customer is actually asking — sometimes the main question is buried at the end.
	•	Check if the question is covered by one of our reference documents (see Section 2 above).
	•	If yes: draft a reply using the relevant information. Keep it friendly and on-brand. See Section 5 for tone guidance.
	•	If no: this is a new question we have not been asked before. Note it down (see Section 6 — New Questions Log) and check with the team before replying.
	•	For order-related issues: log into Shopify first to check the order status before replying.
	•	Send the reply. Mark the email as resolved in Gmail.

4. Common Enquiry Types & What to Do

4.1 Materials & Safety Questions
Examples: Is the strap BPA-free? Is the case hypoallergenic? Is the lume safe?
What to do: Check the Product One-Pager for the specific model. Most safety answers are there. If the question is about a specific certification we have not mentioned before (e.g. EU REACH, RoHS, MRI safety), check with the team before replying.
⚠ Health-conscious customers often ask about BPA, nickel, and skin reactions. The Product One-Pager has the answers for standard questions.

4.2 Engraving
Examples: Can I engrave a message? How many characters? What fonts? Can I change my engraving after ordering?
What to do: Use the Engraving Rate Card. Key things to remember:
	•	Standard text engraving: up to 40 characters including spaces
	•	Pricing: SGD 25 for up to 20 chars, SGD 35 for 21-40 chars
	•	If the customer wants to change/cancel an engraving AFTER ordering:
	•	Within 1 hour of order: amend for free — update the order note in Shopify
	•	1-24 hours after order: SGD 15 amendment fee — check with team before confirming
	•	After production has started: not possible — let customer know politely
	•	Logo or custom artwork: flag for team review, ask customer to send vector file
⚠ Engraved items cannot be returned unless there is a manufacturing defect. Remind customers to double-check spelling before confirming.

4.3 Strap Questions
Examples: Which straps fit my watch? Do you have a rubber strap? Is the strap BPA-free?
What to do: Check the Product One-Pager for strap compatibility. All current models use 20mm lug width and are compatible with any standard 20mm strap.
	•	FKM rubber straps: recommended for sports and sensitive skin
	•	Nylon NATO: lightweight, casual
	•	Titanium bracelet: premium, heavier
	•	Leather: dress wear, not recommended for water activities
If customer asks about strap recycling or sustainability certifications — we do not have this information yet. Note it down in the New Questions Log.

4.5 Order / Shipping / Tracking
What to do: Always check Shopify first before replying. Do not guess order status.
	•	Order status: log into Shopify, search by order number or email, copy tracking number into reply
	•	Tracking not updating after 5+ business days: escalate to shipping carrier, keep customer informed
	•	Wrong item received: ask for a photo, flag to team immediately for replacement
	•	Change delivery address: only possible if order has not been dispatched yet — update manually in Shopify
	•	Cancel order: if unfulfilled in Shopify, cancel and refund. If already shipped, ask customer to return it when it arrives.
	•	Customs duties: customer is responsible for import duties in their own country — we cannot predict the amount

4.6 Returns & Warranty
Key rules:
	•	Returns accepted within 14 days for unworn, unaltered items
	•	Engraved items: non-returnable unless there is a manufacturing defect
	•	Warranty: 2 years on movement. Does NOT cover physical damage, water damage from misuse, or strap wear.
	•	Warranty claim: ask customer to send photos or video of the defect, then flag to team

5. Tone & Brand Voice
When writing replies, keep these principles in mind:
	•	Friendly but not overly casual — we are a premium brand
	•	Direct — answer the question clearly, do not pad with filler
	•	Helpful — if we cannot help directly, point them somewhere useful
	•	Never promise what you are not sure about — check first

Good example reply opening:
"Hi [Name], thanks for reaching out! Happy to help with that."
Avoid:
	•	Starting every reply with "Great question!"
	•	Using overly formal language ("Dear Sir/Madam")
	•	Making promises about timelines you cannot guarantee

6. New Questions Log
If a customer asks something we have never been asked before and it is not in any of our reference documents, do the following:
	•	Reply to the customer honestly — "Let me check on that and get back to you."
	•	Add the question to the New Questions Log spreadsheet (Google Drive > CS Team > New Questions Log).
	•	Discuss with the team at the weekly CS check-in.
	•	Once we have an answer, update the FAQ document so we can answer it faster next time.

7. Escalation — When to Ask for Help
Escalate to the team lead whenever:
	•	A customer is angry or threatening a chargeback
	•	A warranty claim involves significant damage or potential product defect
	•	A refund has been outstanding for more than 10 business days
	•	A corporate or bulk order enquiry comes in (more than 5 units)
	•	You are not sure of the answer and cannot find it in the reference documents
	•	A media or press enquiry comes in — forward to marketing@boldr.co
